Privacy & website analytics

This notice explains the limited analytics planned for this website. The analytics service is optional and will collect events only after it has been configured by the site owner.

What the dashboard measures

When enabled, the site records page views and clicks on WhatsApp, phone, email and Facebook links. It groups counts by date, broad device type and, when Cloudflare provides it, country. It does not collect form contents, names, phone numbers, email addresses or precise location.

Browser identifier and retention

To estimate unique and returning visitors, this browser stores a random identifier in local storage. The analytics service stores a keyed HMAC of that identifier, rather than the original value; this is pseudonymous data, not a direct identity. Records are retained for up to 400 days. Clear this site's browser storage to remove the local identifier.

A short-lived keyed HMAC of the request IP address is used to limit automated event submissions and sign-in attempts. The analytics database does not store raw IP addresses. Cloudflare may process connection information to operate its service under its own terms.

Your analytics choice

Checking this browser…

Opting out stops future analytics from this browser. Existing aggregate records remain until their normal deletion.
